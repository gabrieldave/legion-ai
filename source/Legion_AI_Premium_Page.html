Questions traders ask us

Does Legion AI trade for me?

No. It is an analysis panel. It reads the market and your account, but every trade is your decision. It never opens, changes or closes an order.

Do I need experience?

Basic MetaTrader 4 knowledge is enough. Every number has a tooltip that explains it in simple words, and the overall reading sums everything up in one sentence.

Which markets does it work on?

Any symbol your MT4 broker offers: Gold, Bitcoin, S&P 500, Nasdaq, US30, forex pairs and more. The Historical Drops tool includes crash history for Gold, Bitcoin, S&P 500, Nasdaq and US30.

Will it work with my broker?

If your broker gives you MetaTrader 4, yes. Legion AI runs inside MT4 and only uses the price data your platform already has.

Does it run on my phone?

The panel runs on MT4 for desktop (Windows, or a Mac or VPS that runs MT4). Your phone gets the alerts through MT4 push notifications.

Can it guarantee profits?

No tool can. Legion AI helps you see the market and your risk more clearly. Results always depend on your decisions, your position size and the market.

Will it slow down MetaTrader?

It only redraws what changed instead of repainting everything on every tick, so it stays light even with all fourteen tools on.

Is it in English?

Yes. The whole panel, including tooltips, buttons and alerts, is in English by default, with Spanish as an option.
